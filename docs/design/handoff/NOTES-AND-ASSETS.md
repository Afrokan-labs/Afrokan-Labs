# Afrokan Labs site: review notes and asset checklist

These are all the "Show notes" notes from the mockup, grouped by section, with the asset each one needs.
Tick items off as you send them. Drop finished files into `public/images/` with the names suggested below.

## 1. Hero
- The photo is the interim founder headshot inside an arch shape. For the exact reference look, supply a cut-out portrait, so the head rises above the grey circle.
- The right-hand badge ("Ghana-first · AI & software studio, Accra") replaces the reference's "★★★★★ 10 Years Experience". We don't have ratings to show yet.
- [ ] **Asset:** `hero-founder-cutout.png`, a transparent PNG of Franklin, waist up, confident or arms-raised pose, at least 1200px tall.

## 2. Stats strip
- The reference uses big client and revenue counts. These four (3+ systems delivered, 24/7 WhatsApp replies, 100% mobile-first builds, 4-step process) are things we can stand behind today.
- [ ] Optional: real counts to swap in as they grow, such as clients served, messages answered or years running.

## 3. Turning ideas into working software
- Both images are drawn stand-ins. The red light-bulb doodle stays on top of the real photo.

- [ ] **Asset:** `ideas-portrait-tall.jpg`, a black-and-white portrait of a teammate looking thoughtful, about 2:3.

## 4. Our Services

  - [ ] a small photo or company logo
  - [ ] the client's permission to publish
